A new home for Hardwick online.

Six draft templates, each with a home page and a page for every development showing exactly what is for sale. Current and completed developments are kept separate, and the blue and yellow brand colours have been refined so they read as premium rather than loud. All developments, prices and photos are sample content.

Keeping it up to date

Marking a home as sold

All six templates read from one file, assets/data/developments.js. Change a plot from "available" to "sold" and every page updates, including counts and “from” prices.

Finishing a development

Change stage: "current" to "completed" and add the year. The site moves to the completed or previous projects section automatically.

Starting a new site

Copy an existing development block, paste it, and change the name, details, photos and plots. A new development page is created automatically.
