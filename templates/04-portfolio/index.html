About Hardwick

A family builder, rooted in Swadlincote

We keep our developments small so that every home gets our full attention — from the first drawing to the final hand-over. When you buy a Hardwick home, you deal with the family who built it.

Why Hardwick

Local & family-run

We are based in Swadlincote and build within a few miles of our yard. We know every site, every street and every neighbour.

Small by design

Finished properly
